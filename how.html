是同一套机制One system behind all three stories

OpenMousse 从你的日常、学习与对话中积累对你的理解，记住你自己的观点，与朋友一起思考，并在你同意后替你做事。它运行在一台只属于你的云服务器上。OpenMousse builds its understanding of you from your routines, your learning and your conversations. It remembers your own views, thinks with your friends and acts for you once you approve. It runs on a cloud server that is yours alone.

02 · AgentsAgents
