功能

今天页与 04:00 日结

查看待处理、日程与报告，了解 03:45 日结与 04:00 会话重置的运作机制。

打开 OpenMousse app，在底栏第三格「今天」即可查看「今天页」；每天 03:45 的日结与 04:00 的会话重置由服务器在后台自动执行。

OpenMousse 的一天从 04:00 开始计算。今天页将当天需要你动手审批的事项、需要你到场的日程，以及各功能 Agent 送来的报告汇总在一处；凌晨则自动完成前一日的结论沉淀与对话归档。

今天页的构成

今天页自上而下分为三个常驻板块（早上 05:30–13:00 期间，日视图最上方还会单独展示一张起床卡，即起床报告）：

  • •待处理：需要你动手处理的事项。包括等你批准的确认卡、标签为「安全」的安全提醒（例如好友那边发来的话里夹着指令，或 Doorman 扣下的管家回复），以及快到期的待办和截止事项。
  • •日程：当天需要你到场的安排。
  • •报告：各功能 Agent 整理好送来供你阅读的信息。例如睡眠 Agent 生成的睡眠卡，记录昨晚睡了多久、恢复分，以及近 6 天的恢复分走势。

03:45 日结与 04:00 会话重置

系统按你的时区在每天凌晨执行以下两步操作：

  1. 1.03:45 日结：主对话与各功能 Agent 回顾过去一天的对话与数据，把当天的结论以及新得知的关于你的事实、偏好、决定与近况，分别写进各自的独立记忆与世界树。如果主对话发现某件事务被你反复交办，也会在日结后提议新建一个功能 Agent，放进收件箱等你同意。
  2. 2.04:00 会话重置：当天的对话按天归档。聊天记录按天翻阅，每一天的分界线均为 04:00。
i
提示：起床卡的显示时段

起床卡（起床报告）仅在每天 05:30–13:00 出现在日视图最上方，不归入下方的「报告」板块中。

不在这里的东西

  • •会议纪要卡不在「待处理」中：多 Agent 短会结束后，会议纪要卡直接出现在「主对话」里（提供「确认」与「查看会议」操作），不会进入今天页的「待处理」。
  • •Zen 里未存进库的想法不参与 03:45 日结：你在 Zen 中随手记下的想法，或推敲时点击「只记下」的内容，仅保存为本地 Markdown 笔记，不会在 03:45 日结时自动写入世界树或任何 Agent 的记忆。

Features

Today View and 04:00 Settlement

Explore To do, Schedule, and Reports in the Today view, along with the 03:45 daily settlement and 04:00 session reset.

Open the OpenMousse app to view Today in the third tab of the bottom bar; the daily 03:45 settlement and 04:00 session reset run automatically in the background on your server.

A day in OpenMousse starts at 04:00. Today brings together items waiting for your approval, scheduled events you need to attend, and reports delivered by your functional agents; overnight, the system automatically distills the previous day's conclusions and archives the day's conversations.

Structure of the Today view

From top to bottom, Today is divided into three permanent sections (between 05:30 and 13:00, a wake-up card—your morning briefing—is also displayed at the very top of the day view):

  • •To do: Items that require action from you. This includes approval cards waiting for your approval, security alerts tagged Security (for example, a message from a friend's side carrying embedded instructions, or a Butler reply held back by Doorman), and upcoming tasks and deadlines.
  • •Schedule: Events you need to attend that day.
  • •Reports: Information compiled and delivered for your reading by functional agents—for example, a sleep card generated by the sleep agent showing how long you slept last night, your recovery score, and your 6-day recovery trend.

03:45 settlement and 04:00 session reset

Every night, according to your time zone, the system performs two steps:

  1. 1.03:45 settlement: Main chat and each functional agent review the past day's conversations and data, writing the day's conclusions along with newly learned facts, preferences, decisions, and recent updates about you into their respective dedicated memories and the memory tree. If main chat notices a task you have assigned repeatedly, it also proposes creating a new functional agent after settlement and places the proposal in your inbox for approval.
  2. 2.04:00 session reset: The day's conversations are archived by day. Chat history is browsed by day, with 04:00 as the boundary between days.
i
Note: When the wake-up card appears

The wake-up card (morning briefing) appears at the very top of the day view only between 05:30 and 13:00 each day and is not grouped under the Reports section below.

What stays out

  • •Meeting minutes cards do not appear in To do: After a short multi-agent meeting ends, the meeting minutes card appears directly in Main chat (with Confirm and Open actions) and does not go into To do in Today.
  • •Unsaved thoughts in Zen are excluded from the 03:45 settlement: Quick thoughts jotted down in Zen, or notes saved by tapping private note while refining an idea, are stored only as local Markdown notes and are not automatically written to the memory tree or any agent's memory during the 03:45 settlement.

See also

  • •Approvals (Inbox): Learn when approval cards and security alerts are generated and how to handle them.
  • •Memory Tree: Learn what the 03:45 settlement writes to the memory tree.
  • •Agents: Learn the everyday roles of functional agents and how meeting minutes work.
