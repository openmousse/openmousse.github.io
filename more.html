今天的 AI 助手，缺少上下文与行动力Agents fall short on two things: context and action

上下文Context

你在 Claude 里写代码，在其他 AI 平台讨论学习与生活，在 Notion 里记笔记。关于你的上下文分散在各处，每一个新的 AI 都要从零开始了解你。You code with Claude, think through study and life on other AI platforms, and keep notes in Notion. Your context is scattered, so every new AI starts from zero.

行动Action

即使了解你，它也接触不到你的身体状态、日程与邮件，无法在合适的时刻替你做事。Even an AI that knows you has no access to your health data, calendar or inbox, so it can’t act for you at the right moment.

世界树memory treeMemory tree
OpenMousse 的长期记忆层，保存在你的服务器上。它通过 MCP（AI 应用之间共享上下文的开放协议）与各个 AI 平台双向同步：先从你已有的对话中提炼出主干（身份、偏好、习惯、所在地与职业），再按学习、工作、生活、社交四枝持续生长。OpenMousse’s long-term memory, kept on your server. It syncs both ways with your AI platforms over MCP, the open protocol AI apps use to share context. It first distills a trunk from the conversations you already have (who you are, your preferences, habits, location and work), then grows four branches: learning, work, life and social.
