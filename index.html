你在其他 AI 平台上的对话、日常数据、学习内容与个人观点，都汇聚到同一棵世界树。世界树是 OpenMousse 关于你的长期记忆。Your conversations on other AI platforms, your daily data, what you study and the views you hold all grow into one memory tree: OpenMousse’s long-term memory of you.

故事一Story one

起点Starting out

你今年毕业，正在找第一份工作。You graduate this year, and you’re looking for your first job.

这一周有三场面试。它按面试的时间，调整了你的作息与三餐。面试：周二 10:00、周三 14:00、周四 09:30。面试前一晚 23:00 前休息，早餐提前到 07:30。You have three interviews this week. It plans your sleep and meals around them.Interviews: Tue 10:00, Wed 14:00, Thu 09:30. In bed by 23:00 the night before; breakfast moved to 07:30.

你原以为，这就是它的全部用处。You assume that’s all it’s for.

一天深夜，你读到一篇报道：AI 将率先取代初级岗位。Late one night, you read a report: AI will replace entry-level jobs first.

而你投递的，正是这些岗位。Those are exactly the jobs you’ve applied for.

你把一个问题存进 Zen，留待推敲：刚毕业的人，该从哪里起步？You save a question to Zen to think through later: where should a new graduate start?

第二天，室友说：越是这样，越要先进大公司，那里有完整的培养体系。The next day, your roommate says: all the more reason to join a big company first. They know how to train people.

另一位朋友的看法正相反：初级岗位正在减少，不如直接创业。A friend sees it the other way: entry-level jobs are disappearing, so start a company now.

你倾向于创业，却说不清理由。你们约定录一期播客，把这个问题谈透。You lean toward starting up, but you can’t say why. The three of you agree to record a podcast and talk it through.

录制前，它根据你存在 Zen 里的问题拟好了提纲。提纲：AI 接手的，是初级岗位上的哪些工作？大公司的培养体系，培养的是什么？刚毕业的人，该从哪里起步？Before you record, it drafts an outline from the question you saved in Zen.Outline: Which parts of entry-level work will AI take over? What do big-company training programs actually train? Where should a new graduate start?

三个人谈了一个小时，它分得清每个人的声音。You talk for an hour. It can tell all three voices apart.

录完，你第一次说清了自己的理由。它把这段话记进了世界树。你的理由：AI 接手的是初级岗位上的重复工作，接不走的是判断。判断只能在承担后果的决定中练成；创业，从第一天起就要做这样的决定。By the end, you can finally put your reason into words. It saves them to the memory tree.Your reason: AI takes over the repetitive work in entry-level jobs. It can’t take over judgment. Judgment only grows from decisions you have to live with, and a startup asks you to make them from day one.

你决定创业。You decide to start a company.

半年后，你申请一家创业加速器。申请表的第一题是：为什么不先工作几年？Six months later, you apply to an accelerator. The first question on the form: why not work for a few years first?

它起草回答时，把你在播客里的那段话放在了第一段。When it drafts your answer, it opens with what you said on the podcast.

它记住的不只是你的日程，It remembers more than your schedule.

还有你怎么想。It remembers how you think.

故事二Story two

学习Learning

你读大二，这学期修一门统计课。You’re in your second year at university, taking a statistics course.

课件、阅读清单与录播，你都交给了它。You give it the slides, the reading list and the lecture recordings.

每节课后，它请你用自己的话复述一遍。After each class, it asks you to explain the lecture in your own words.

复述到「回归均值」，你卡住了：考得最好的人，下一次为什么多半会考得低一些？You get stuck on regression to the mean: why do the top scorers usually score lower the next time?

它没有直接讲答案，而是问了你一个问题。它问：全班每人抛十次硬币，正面最多的五个人再抛一次，他们还会是前五吗？Instead of giving you the answer, it asks you a question.It asks: if everyone in the class flips a coin ten times, will the five with the most heads still be the top five when they flip again?

你答：不会，他们只是那一次运气好。You answer: no, they just got lucky that time.

你由此想通：成绩是实力加运气，考得最好的那一次，运气往往也最好。例如：实力 80 分，那一次运气加了 12 分，考了 92 分。Then it clicks: a score is skill plus luck, and the best score usually came with the best luck.For example: skill worth 80 points plus 12 points of luck makes 92.

下一次运气回到平常，成绩就回到实力本来的位置。他们并没有变差。实力仍是 80 分，运气只加了 1 分，考了 81 分。Next time, luck returns to normal and the score falls back to the level of their skill. They didn’t get worse.Skill is still 80, luck adds 1 point, and the score is 81.

你又想到财富：最富有的人，往往也是运气最好的人。运气传不下去，财富也很难一直留在同一批人手里。You think of wealth, too: the richest people are often the luckiest. Luck can’t be inherited, so wealth rarely stays with the same people for long.

它把这两段话记进了世界树。It saves both thoughts to the memory tree.

两个月后，你拿到第一笔实习收入，打算拿出一部分投资。Two months later, your first internship pay arrives, and you decide to invest some of it.

排行榜上，去年收益第一的基金涨了 68%。你正准备买入。Top of the rankings is a fund that returned 68% last year. You’re about to buy it.

它把你两个月前的那段话放了回来。It brings back what you wrote two months ago.

你没有追买去年的第一名，而是分散投资了一只覆盖整个市场的指数基金。You don’t chase last year’s winner. You put the money into an index fund that covers the whole market.

一年后，那只基金回到了中游。去年第 1 名，今年第 41 名，共 80 只。A year later, that fund is back in the middle of the pack.First last year; 41st of 80 this year.

课本上的定义，谁都查得到。Anyone can look up the textbook definition.

你自己想通的那句话，只有它记得。What you worked out for yourself, only it remembers.
