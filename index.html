A study desk built around your courses按你的课，定制一个学习台

Start a course from whatever you have and tell it how you like to learn. Each session gets its own notes and study path, with flashcards, a quiz and an explainer video if you want them, and a chat that answers only from that session's materials.手上有什么就从什么开始加课，再告诉它你喜欢怎么学。每节课有自己的学习页和学习路线，闪卡、小测、讲解视频要就加，旁边的对话只按这节课的材料回答。

Adding a course加一门课

  1. 1Tell it about the course说说这门课The name, how it's examined, how you like to learn.课名、怎么考、你喜欢怎么学。
  2. 2Show what you have你手上有什么A syllabus, slides, your course site (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard) or nothing yet. It tells you where to find the rest.大纲、课件、课程网站（Canvas、Moodle、Blackboard），或者还什么都没有。缺的它告诉你去哪拿。
  3. 3Check each session核对每一节Sessions, dates, readings and deadlines, laid out for you to fix in chat.每一节、日期、阅读、截止都列出来，哪里不对在对话里说。
  4. 4Add the files放材料Drop them in. It sorts them into sessions and flags what's missing.拖进来就行，它按节归好，缺什么会标出来。
  5. 5Build生成Notes and a study path for every session that's complete.材料齐了的每一节，出学习页和学习路线。
  • Study path学习路线Each session opens on 5–8 steps pointing at the slide pages, note sections, readings and lecture moments to go through. Tick them off as you go.每节课一打开就是 5–8 步，指到该看的课件页、学习页小节、阅读和录播时间点，做完一步勾一步。
  • Self-test自测Write your answer first, then reveal the reference beside it. Mark it right, close or missed, or ask it to check yours.先写自己的答案，再和参考答案并排对照；自己判对了、差一点、没答上，或者让它看看你的答案。
  • Review复习Whatever you got wrong or left out in a Feynman check waits at the top of that session until you've gone over it.费曼对照里讲错、讲漏的，挂在那一节最上面，复习过了再划掉。
  • On your phone手机上Review, flashcards, the quiz and the path from the study Agent's card. Long reading stays on the big screen.从学习 Agent 的卡片进去：复习、闪卡、小测、学习路线打勾。长篇阅读留在电脑上。

What you learn at the desk doesn't stay there:在学习台学到的，不会只留在学习台：

  1. Think it over想一想Jot what clicked, or didn't, in Zen. When you talk it through, it brings in the session's slides and your older notes.课上想到的、没想通的，先丢进 Zen。点「聊聊」时，它会带上这节课的课件和你以前的笔记。
  2. Explain it back讲回来Explain the session out loud in Feynman mode. It checks you against the slides, and what you missed becomes flashcards.用费曼模式把这节课讲一遍。它拿课件对你讲的，漏掉的变成闪卡。
  3. Talk it through with friends和朋友聊Share a session's summary. Classmates can read the study notes you mark as shareable, and a podcast together before the exam leaves each of you with minutes.把一节课的要点分享出去；同学能看你标了可分享的学习笔记。考前约同学录一期播客，每人一份纪要。
  4. Keep what matters留下该留的Your own takeaways go into your vault, not the raw slides. The memory tree learns what you're studying and what you keep missing, so every AI you use knows it too.你自己的心得进库，课件原文不进。世界树记住你在学什么、哪里老出错，你用的每个 AI 也都知道。

Why OpenMousse为什么做 OpenMousse

When Meta's Muse was everywhere, the author wrote a post about it: Muse is a claw in an app shell, and the trouble is that it's centralized. You can't pick the model, the memory starts from zero, and your data can't leave. OpenMousse is the answer to that post.Muse 刷屏的那几天，作者写了一篇文章：Muse 其实是一只套着 app 外壳的 claw，问题在于太中心化，模型选不了，记忆从零开始，数据也带不走。OpenMousse 就是那篇文章的回答。
