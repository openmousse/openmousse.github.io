数据与安全Security

数据在哪里，经过哪里，由谁批准Where your data lives, where it goes and who approves

OpenMousse 运行在一台只属于你的服务器上。本页说明数据存放在哪里、哪些数据会离开这台服务器，以及哪些操作必须等你批准。OpenMousse runs on a server that is yours alone. This page explains where your data is stored, what leaves that server and which actions wait for your approval.

01

数据存放在哪里Where your data is stored

所有数据都保存在你的服务器上。下表列出每一类数据的存放位置、谁能读取，以及如何删除。Everything is stored on your server. Here is where each kind of data lives, who can read it and how to delete it.

  • 对话与附件Conversations and attachments
    存放位置Stored in
    你服务器上的数据库与文件夹A database and folders on your server
    谁能读取Readable by
    你配对过的设备，以及服务器上你的 AgentDevices you have paired, and your agents on the server
    如何删除To delete
    在 app 中删除，或在设置中清空全部数据Delete them in the app, or erase everything in Settings
  • 世界树Memory tree
    存放位置Stored in
    Markdown 笔记，一条记忆一篇Markdown notes, one per memory
    谁能读取Readable by
    你，以及你接入的 AI 平台；每个平台各用一条可以撤销的私密地址You, and the AI platforms you connect, each through its own private address you can revoke
    如何删除To delete
    在 app 中让它遗忘某一条，或直接删除笔记In the app, ask OpenMousse to forget a memory, or delete the note itself
  • 健康数据Health data
    存放位置Stored in
    你服务器上的数据库；生殖健康类数据不申请、不同步A database on your server; reproductive health data is never requested or synced
    谁能读取Readable by
    服务器上你的 AgentYour agents on the server
    如何删除To delete
    在手机的系统设置中停止共享；已同步的数据随「清空全部数据」删除Stop sharing in your phone’s settings; synced data is removed when you erase everything
  • 密码与登录凭证Passwords and sign-in credentials
    存放位置Stored in
    由 Doorman 单独保管Doorman’s separate store
    谁能读取Readable by
    只有 Doorman；代办 Agent 只拿到占位符Doorman alone; the Errands agent only ever holds a placeholder
    如何删除To delete
    在设置中移除Remove them in Settings
  • 分享与好友消息Shares and messages with friends
    存放位置Stored in
    你和好友各自的服务器Your server and your friend’s
    谁能读取Readable by
    你与这位好友You and that friend
    如何删除To delete
    收回分享后，链接与卡片随即失效；删除好友即停止往来Withdraw a share to disable its link and card; remove a friend to end the exchange
02

离开服务器的数据What leaves your server

OpenMousse 按设计只建立以下连接，每一项都写明发送什么、由你怎样控制。你的 claw 另外接入的渠道与工具（例如聊天软件、网页搜索），按它们各自的设置运行。By design, OpenMousse makes only these connections. Each is listed with what is sent and how you control it. Channels and tools your claw connects separately, such as chat apps or web search, follow their own settings.

  • 模型服务Model services
    发送内容What is sent
    每一轮的消息与回答所需的记忆，发往你选择的服务，包括你设置的备用模型Each turn’s messages and the memory needed to answer, sent to the services you choose, including any fallback models you set
    你的控制How you control it
    随时更换服务，或改用自有 API 密钥Switch services at any time, or use your own API key
  • 推送通知Push notifications
    发送内容What is sent
    标题与一段预览，经 Expo 以及 Apple 或 Google 的推送服务送达A title and a short preview, delivered through Expo and Apple’s or Google’s push service
    你的控制How you control it
    每一类通知都可以设为响铃、静音或不推送Set each kind of notification to ring, arrive silently or stay off
  • 语音转写Voice transcription
    发送内容What is sent
    录音，发往你选择的转写服务Your recordings, sent to the transcription service you choose
    你的控制How you control it
    不录音就不会发送Nothing is sent unless you record
  • 你接入的应用Apps you connect
    发送内容What is sent
    例如 Notion：由你的服务器在你授权的范围内读写Data your server reads and writes in apps such as Notion, within the access you granted
    你的控制How you control it
    每个工具可设为自动、先问我或关闭，随时可以断开Set each tool to automatic, ask first or off, and disconnect at any time
  • 接入世界树的 AI 平台AI platforms connected to your memory tree
    发送内容What is sent
    各平台经自己的私密地址读写世界树Your memory tree, which each platform reads and writes through its own private address
    你的控制How you control it
    随时撤销任一平台的地址Revoke any platform’s address at any time
  • 代办的网页请求Web requests from Errands
    发送内容What is sent
    只经 Doorman 发出：读取直接放行；提交、发送、登录与付款等你批准Requests sent only through Doorman. Reads pass straight through; submissions, messages, sign-ins and payments wait for your approval
    你的控制How you control it
    提交、发送、登录与付款都由你批准You approve every submission, message, sign-in and payment
  • 好友Friends
    发送内容What is sent
    只有你分享的内容，并先遮盖隐私信息；每条带签名，直接发往好友的服务器Only what you share, with private details hidden first; each message is signed and goes straight to your friend’s server
    你的控制How you control it
    收回分享，或删除好友Withdraw a share, or remove the friend

自主部署时，我们看不到你的任何数据。OpenMousse 账号是可选的，只保存邮箱、名字，以及你连接过的服务器（名称、地址、类型与最近一次连接的时间）；从不保存令牌、对话、记忆或密码。app 中没有统计或追踪 SDK。When you self-host, we can’t see any of your data. An OpenMousse account is optional and holds only your email address, your name and the servers you have connected (name, address, type and when each last connected). It never holds tokens, conversations, memories or passwords. The app has no analytics or tracking SDKs.

04

外来的文字不会触发操作Outside text can’t trigger actions

网页或其他 Agent 发来的文字，可能藏着让 AI 执行操作的指令。OpenMousse 让这类文字无法触发任何操作：好友的名片 Agent 与其他 Agent 发来的消息，由不能使用任何工具的模型调用来回答；网页只由代办读取，代办发出的每个请求都要经过 Doorman，提交、发送、登录与付款都要等你批准。Text from web pages or other agents can hide instructions meant to make an AI act. OpenMousse makes sure such text can’t trigger any action. Messages from friends’ card agents and other agents are answered by model calls that have no tools at all. Only the Errands agent reads web pages, and every request it sends passes through Doorman, where submissions, messages, sign-ins and payments wait for your approval.

05

自主部署与托管Self-hosted and hosted

自主部署Self-hosted

服务器、密钥与数据都由你掌握，我们没有任何访问途径。You hold the server, the keys and the data. We have no way in.

托管服务Hosted

我们为你运行一台专属服务器，不与他人共用。我们不查看你的数据；只有在你为排查问题明确授权时，运维人员才会登录，并留下你能查看的记录。随时可以整机迁移到你自己的服务器，或一键清空。We run a dedicated server for you; it is never shared. We don’t look at your data. An engineer signs in only when you explicitly allow access to fix a problem, and every such session is logged where you can see it. Move the whole server to your own at any time, or erase it in one step.
